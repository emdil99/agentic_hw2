Who it's for

People who buy fitness supplements and want to know what is really in the tub before trusting the front of it.

Suggested use
  • Name the brand and product, e.g. MTN OPS Creatine Unflavored.
  • Ask about amounts, warnings, directions, or two products side by side.
  • Ask about the whole database: which product has the most or least of something, or how many list an ingredient.
  • Open Show work under any answer to see each search and lookup the agent ran.
